# Testimonial drafts for /discounts

**Status: UNAPPROVED. Do not publish any quote on this page until the business has said yes in writing.**

These are *drafts written by us* for each business to approve, edit, or reject. That's a normal,
legitimate way to get a testimonial. The words can start as ours, the sign-off has to be theirs.
What we can't do is publish them as-is and hope nobody checks.

Why this matters concretely, not just in principle:

- The FTC's **Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials** (16 CFR Part 465, in force Oct 2024)
  bans testimonials the named endorser never made, with civil penalties per violation.
- Each of these is a **real, findable business**. Any of them can see the page and object.
- **Chick-fil-A is a trademark holder**, and the Tate Center location is a licensed campus unit.
  The local operator almost certainly *cannot* authorize use of the Chick-fil-A name and marks in
  a third party's marketing, and that call sits with corporate brand/legal. Treat this one as the
  slowest of the three, and be ready to run it as "A national chicken chain on campus" or drop it.

Once a business approves, move the entry into `TESTIMONIALS` in
`src/components/discounts/DiscountTestimonials.tsx`, drop the photo in
`public/images/testimonials/`, and the section renders itself. Keep the approval email.

## 1. Backroads Brewhouse, Fort Myers, FL
**Angle: filling slow hours**

> "Monday and Tuesday nights used to be dead. We put a Bizzy-only deal up and now there are
> students in here on the nights we used to think about closing early. It filled the hours we
> couldn't give away."

Attribution needed: first name + role (e.g. "Dana R., Owner"). Ask which they prefer.

## 2. Chick-fil-A, UGA Tate Center, Athens, GA
**Angle: 5-minute setup, no hardware**

> "Setup took about five minutes. No new hardware, nothing to install, no codes for my team to
> memorize. A student shows the deal, we tap once, done. It hasn't slowed a single line down."

Attribution needed: operator name + title. **Also needs corporate brand approval. See above.**

## 3. Fieldhouse, East Lansing, MI

> "We run ours as a monthly exclusive, so it resets and pulls the same students back in. They're
> not coming once for a discount, they're turning into regulars. That's the part I didn't expect."

Attribution needed: first name + role.
