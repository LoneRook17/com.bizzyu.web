# Known issues & situational answers

Living file — prune entries when fixed. The bot treats these as current truth.

## A business isn't honoring a listed deal

If a student says a business refused a deal shown in the app (e.g. "they said they
don't do that anymore"): apologize, don't blame the business, thank them for
flagging it. Collect the business name, the deal, and when it happened, and ask
them to email support@bizzyu.com (or offer to have the team review it). The team
will verify the listing with the business and update the app.

## Bought a line skip on the website (not in the app)

Line skips bought through web checkout may **not appear in the app's Wallet** if
the buyer didn't have an account at purchase time. The QR code in the
**confirmation email** is the ticket — it works at the door exactly the same.
If they can't find the email (check spam), escalate with the purchase email
address.

## Add to Apple Wallet button missing from a receipt email

Some receipt emails may not show the Add to Apple Wallet button. The QR in the
email and in the app's Wallet tab still works at the door. This is known and
being improved.

## Accidentally claimed a deal

Covered in `10-students.md`: claims can't be undone in-app. For daily/weekly/
monthly deals, it unlocks again on schedule. For one-time deals or when it burned
one of a free user's weekly claims, escalate to support@bizzyu.com — the team can
review these case by case. Do not promise a reset.

## Transferred to a different school

School changes are supported. If the option isn't visible in Profile → Settings,
email support@bizzyu.com with the new school name and the team will switch it.

## My school isn't on Bizzy

Students and hosts can request a school on the website at /request-school
(also linked from signup campus pickers and unpublished campus pages). That
form emails the team. Adding the college to the live list is a Laravel
university-list / catalog change, not something the website publishes itself.
Requesting boosts the campus's priority.
