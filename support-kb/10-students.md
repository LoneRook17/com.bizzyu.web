# Student side

Grounded in a code-behavior trace (2026-07-03). Re-verify when flows change.

## Accounts & login

- Signup is by **phone number**: you get a 6-digit text code that **expires after
  5 minutes**. Requesting a new code invalidates the old one — always enter the
  newest code received.
- Code not arriving: check the number is correct, wait a minute (carrier delays),
  then tap resend. Still nothing after a few tries → email support@bizzyu.com with
  the phone number.
- One account per phone number and per email. "Phone number already exists" or
  "Email already exists" means an account is already registered with it — log in
  instead, or escalate if the user says it isn't theirs.
- **Changing schools** (e.g. after transferring) is supported — the user's school
  can be updated on their account. If they can't find the option in Profile →
  Settings, have them email support@bizzyu.com with the new school and it will be
  changed.
- **Profile editing**: name, email, phone, password, and photo can be changed in
  Profile → Settings.
- **Deleting an account**: available in the app (Profile → Settings). Deletion is
  permanent; it removes personal info and frees the phone number and email for a
  future signup. Purchase records are retained where required.

## Deals — claiming and cooldowns

- To use a deal: tap **Claim** in the app and show the claimed deal to staff
  in-store. Claiming is the redemption — there's no separate code.
- **Claims cannot be undone.** If someone claims by accident, the app cannot
  un-claim it (not even the support team can from chat) — apologize, and for
  one-time deals or hit limits, escalate to support@bizzyu.com for review.
- Reset schedule by deal frequency:
  - **Daily** deals reset at midnight.
  - **Weekly** deals reset at the start of the next week (Sunday).
  - **Monthly** deals reset at the start of the next month.
  - **One-time / limited-time** deals never reset — one claim ever.
  - **Anytime** deals can always be claimed.
  After a claim, periodic deals show a countdown until they unlock again. The timer
  is enforced by the server — there is no way to claim early.
- **Deal hours**: some deals are only claimable during hours the business sets.
  Outside those hours the app says the deal isn't available right now.
- **Free vs Premium limits** (at participating schools): free users get their first
  claim unrestricted, then **2 claims per week**, and can't claim premium-only or
  one-time/limited-time deals. **Bizzy Premium** removes all of those limits.
- "Deal recently claimed" = the deal is in cooldown for this user.

## Bizzy Premium

- Subscription through **Apple** (in-app purchase): monthly or yearly, with a
  **7-day free trial**. Promo codes can be redeemed in the app.
- Unlimited deal claims, access to premium-only deals.
- Manage or cancel: iPhone **Settings → [name] → Subscriptions → Bizzy** (Apple
  controls billing). Refunds for subscription charges go through Apple
  (reportaproblem.apple.com), not Bizzy.

## Tickets

- Buy from the Events tab: pick ticket type + quantity, pay by card or Apple Pay.
  A **service fee** is added at checkout.
- After purchase the ticket appears in the **Wallet tab** (QR code) and a receipt
  email arrives with the QR and, where available, an **Add to Apple Wallet** button.
- At the door, staff scan the QR. Each ticket scans once — a re-scan shows
  "already redeemed."
- **Scheduled tickets have scan windows**: the QR is only valid between the ticket's
  valid-from and valid-until times (shown in the event's local timezone). Scanning
  before or after the window is rejected.
- **Ticket transfer to another person is not supported** in the app.
- **All sales are final** — individual refunds aren't available. Exception: if the
  business **cancels the event**, every buyer is automatically refunded in full
  (price + fees) to their original payment method via Stripe; banks typically post
  it within several business days. A cancelled ticket's QR stops scanning and any
  Apple Wallet pass is invalidated.
- Paid but no ticket showing / charged twice / unknown charge → **escalate
  immediately** to support@bizzyu.com with the email on the account, event name,
  and purchase time.

## Line skips

- Skip-the-line passes for bars/nightlife, **21+**, sold for specific upcoming
  nights. The price **includes cover** — you don't pay again at the door, and entry
  is guaranteed while the night's window is open.
- After purchase you get a QR (in the app Wallet and by email). Show it at the
  door; staff scan it with their phone camera.
- One-time use; valid only for the specific night/time window purchased.
- Same refund policy as tickets: final sale unless the venue cancels the night, in
  which case unredeemed passes are automatically refunded in full.

## Profile & leaderboard

- Profile shows lifetime money saved and deals claimed.
- The **leaderboard** ranks students per school, monthly, by amount saved.
