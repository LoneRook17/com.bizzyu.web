# Hosting events (any student can)

Grounded 2026-08-31 against the live 5.0 host flow. Re-verify when flows
change.

Any Bizzy user can host their own event — a party, a show, a fundraiser, a
club night. Hosting is not reserved for businesses. The difference is only
in how the event goes live (see Review below).

## Starting an event

- The entry point is **"+ Host an event"** on the **Events tab** (top of the
  page), or **"Host an event"** in **Profile**. It is visible to everyone.
- Users who are on a business's team create **as that business** (if they
  belong to more than one, the app asks which). Everyone else creates as
  themselves — a personal host.
- Personal hosts create **named events** (green). **Weekly Cover is for
  venues/businesses only** — a personal host won't see that option, and
  that is expected, not a bug.
- The host fills in: event name, description, date and time, location,
  flyer image, an optional 21+ toggle, and ticket tiers. Tickets can be
  **free ($0) or paid**, each tier with its own price, quantity, and
  max-per-person.

## Review — when does it go live?

- A personal host's event is **reviewed by the Bizzy team before it goes
  live**. This applies to free and paid events alike. The host gets a
  **text message when it's approved**. Don't promise how long review takes.
- Approved businesses (and hosts the Bizzy team has marked as trusted from
  past events) publish **immediately**, with an automated content check on
  the name, description, and flyer — if something gets flagged, the event
  goes to a short review first.
- Events can also be saved as a **draft** and finished later.
- Connecting Stripe does **not** skip review. Publishing approval and
  getting paid are separate things.

## Getting paid as a personal host

- A host can sell **paid tickets before setting up any payment account**.
  Bizzy **holds the money** from those sales, and the app shows the held
  earnings.
- To receive the money, the host connects **Stripe** from the app (Profile →
  payment/Stripe setup). Once their Stripe account is active, held earnings
  are released to it, and future sales follow. Stripe then deposits to
  their bank on its own schedule — never promise a number of days.
- The **service fee** is added on top of the ticket price and paid by the
  buyer; the host receives the full price they set.

## After the event is created — the Host tab

- Once someone hosts (or joins an event team / business team), a **Host tab
  appears** in the app's bottom bar. That is where their events live:
  edit details, manage tickets and promo codes, add co-hosts, see sales
  and check-ins, send announcements, cancel.
- Regular students who never host simply don't have this tab — four tabs
  (Deals, Events, Wallet, Profile) is normal.
- Every **named event** gets a **6-digit door code** the host can share so
  a friend at the door can scan tickets with the Bizzy app, no account
  needed (see the door-code notes in the business article — the same
  applies to personal hosts).

## Cancelling

Same policy as business events: cancelling refunds every buyer in full
(price + fees) automatically through Stripe. If money would have to be
refunded, the cancellation goes to the Bizzy team for approval first; free
events cancel immediately. Events that already ended can't be cancelled.
