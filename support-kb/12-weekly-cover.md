# Weekly Cover (student side)

Grounded 2026-08-25 against the live Weekly Cover product. Re-verify when
flows change.

Weekly Cover is a **series of nights** at a venue, not a one-off named event.
Students buy **Cover** (or a Skip the Line option on that same night) for **one
specific night**. It is not a pass for the whole run, a membership, or a
subscription.

## How to tell it from an event or a line skip

Identify the product from what the app/wallet actually is, **never from the
title**. A night named "Weekly Cover Launch Party" or anything ending in
"Cover" can still be a normal event.

- **Weekly Cover / Cover** — `product_kind` is `weekly_cover` (older payloads
  may only say `access_kind` door_access / weekly_cover). Pink in Happening
  Tonight and the Events tab. Guest copy says Cover or Weekly Cover, **not**
  "door access".
- **Named event** — `product_kind` is `event`. Green. Concerts, DJ nights,
  Trivia Tuesdays, themed shows. Staff scan with the in-app Bizzy scanner
  (or a 6-digit door code on the login screen).
- **Line skip** — the standalone nightlife product bought from a venue's
  line-skip listing. Includes cover. Scanned with the phone's regular camera.
  Not the same thing as a Weekly Cover tier named "Skip the Line".

If it isn't clear, have them check the **Wallet** tab. Don't guess.

## Buying and the Wallet

- Find it on the **Events** tab (pink Weekly Cover rows) or on the venue's
  page under **Happening Tonight** / that night's date.
- Pick the night you want, pick Cover or Skip the Line if both are offered,
  pay. A service fee is added at checkout.
- After purchase the QR is in the **Wallet** tab and in the receipt email.
  Valid for **that night only**. Buying Thursday does not get you in Friday.
- Often 21+. The night or the tier can require it.

## At the door

- Staff or the guest open the QR with **any phone camera** (not the Bizzy
  in-app scanner). Tap **Check In**. No staff login.
- Check-in opens closer to doors and closes after that night's window. If
  it isn't open yet, the page says so. Don't tell them they'll be let in
  anyway.
- A re-scan after a successful check-in shows already scanned.
- Named-event tickets still use the in-app scanner. Don't send a Cover QR
  to "scan it in the Bizzy app" or a named-event ticket to camera Check In.

## Tiers

A night can sell **Cover** (entry) and/or **Skip the Line** (faster entry;
the venue chooses whether cover is included). Read what's included at
checkout. A "Skip the Line" option **inside Weekly Cover** is still Cover
for that night, scanned the Weekly Cover way. The standalone **line skip**
product is bought from the venue's line-skip listing.

## Refunds

Same as tickets: all sales are final unless the venue **cancels that
night**. If they cancel, buyers are refunded automatically (price + fees)
to the original payment method. Don't promise when the money lands. Paid
but no QR / charged twice → escalate immediately with the email on the
account, venue, and night.

## Events tab, Happening Tonight, and Venues

- **Happening Tonight** and the **Events** tab: Weekly Cover nights are
  Weekly Cover (pink). Named events are events (green). Same night can
  show both kinds. Color and product kind decide, not the title.
- **Events → Venues**: that list is venues that have **upcoming named
  events or Weekly Cover**. A place that only posts **deals** (a deal
  restaurant) does not show there just because it has a deal.

## What not to say

- Don't call Weekly Cover "door access" or "an event".
- Don't call it a subscription, membership, or season pass.
- Don't treat a title ending in Cover as proof it is Weekly Cover.
- Don't send Weekly Cover to the in-app scanner or a 6-digit door code
  flow. Those stay for **named events**.
- Don't invent extra products (surge, a separate "door access" guest
  product, Night Cover as its own listing). If someone describes
  something that isn't here, escalate.
