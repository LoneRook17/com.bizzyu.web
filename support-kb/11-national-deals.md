# National deals (student side)

Grounded 2026-08-31 against the live National Deals feature. Re-verify when
flows change.

National deals are **student discounts from national brands** (streaming,
food chains, retail, subscriptions). They are not tied to any campus or local
venue — every student on Bizzy sees the same national catalog, on top of the
local deals for their own school. The catalog is curated by the Bizzy team;
businesses cannot create national deals from the dashboard, and students
cannot submit them.

## Where they live in the app

- On the **Deals tab**, at the top there is a **Local / National toggle**.
  Local shows the student's campus deals; National shows the brand catalog.
- The National view has **category chips** (food, streaming, and so on) to
  filter, and a **Claimed** list for deals the student has already used.
- Some national deals can also appear as cards inside the main Deals feed.
- The catalog can be **browsed without being logged in**; opening a deal
  requires an account.

## How a national deal is used — different from local deals

This is the part people mix up. A **local deal** is claimed in the app and
shown to staff in-store. A **national deal** is redeemed on the **brand's
own website**:

1. The student taps the deal, then taps **Open Deal**.
2. The app opens the brand's website (in the browser). Any signup, code
   entry, or checkout happens **on the brand's site**, not in Bizzy.
3. There is **no QR code and nothing to show in person** for a national
   deal, and no claim countdown.

Later, on returning to the app, Bizzy may ask **"did you claim it?"** about
a deal the student opened:

- Answering **yes** moves the deal to the **Claimed** list. It leaves the
  main National feed but stays reachable under Claimed, because brand
  discounts are usually reusable (the student can open it again from there).
- Answering **no** (or dismissing the prompt) leaves the deal in the feed.
- The answer is self-reported and only organizes the student's own list.
  It can't be wrong in a way that costs anything, so don't escalate over it.

## National deals never touch local-deal limits

These rules are firm, and they are the answers to the common confusions:

- Opening or claiming a national deal **does NOT use a weekly free claim**.
  The free-user limit (first claim free, then 2 per week) applies to
  **local campus deals only**.
- National deals are **free for every student**. There is no premium-only
  national deal, and Bizzy Premium does not change what's in the National
  catalog. (Premium's unlimited claims apply to local deals.)
- National deals do **not** add to lifetime savings, deals-claimed counts,
  or the **leaderboard**. Those track local campus deals only.
- Local deal cooldowns (daily/weekly/monthly) do **not** apply to national
  deals. Whether a brand offer can be used again is up to the brand and
  its website.

## Troubleshooting

- **The deal won't open / "this deal has no link yet"**: the outbound link
  is missing or broken. Apologize and escalate to support@bizzyu.com with
  the brand and deal name. Links are checked automatically, but a brand can
  change its page at any time.
- **The brand's website rejected the offer**: the offer terms live with the
  brand, so Bizzy can't force it. Collect the brand, the deal name, and
  what the site said, and escalate so the team can verify the listing.
- **A claimed deal disappeared from the feed**: it moved to the Claimed
  list (Deals tab → National → Claimed). It can be opened again from there.
