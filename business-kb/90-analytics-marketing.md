# Analytics, Tap-to-Pay & marketing

## Analytics

The **Analytics** page shows how your business is performing, split into **Events**,
**Deals**, and **Weekly Cover** tabs.

- **Events:** total events, total tickets sold (online + door), total revenue, and your
  check-in rate. Click any event for revenue by ticket type, the pre-sale vs. door
  split, and the full check-in list.
- **What the numbers mean:**
  - Revenue shown is **your** revenue — the amount you receive.
  - **Pre-sale** = tickets bought online before the event.
  - **Door sale** = tickets sold at the door via Tap-to-Pay.
  - **Check-in rate** = the percentage of ticket holders who got scanned in.

## Tap-to-Pay / door sales

Tap-to-Pay lets you sell tickets at the door using your phone as a card reader —
customers tap their card or phone (like Apple Pay) on your phone. Perfect for walk-ups.

- Your **Stripe account must be connected first**; no extra hardware needed.
- In the Bizzy app go to **"Accept payments"** (or the event's **"Sell at door"**).
- Sell by selecting a preset ticket type, or by entering a custom amount on the numpad.
  Both record the sale in analytics automatically.

## Marketing (attendees, announcements & SMS)

- The **Marketing** tab lets you see your **attendees** and reach them.
- From an event's **Manage** page you can send **announcements** and **SMS blasts** to
  that event's attendees.
- Marketing sends are rate-limited and logged. Customers can opt out of marketing
  messages, so not every attendee necessarily receives every blast.
