# Managing tickets

Manage tickets from an event's **Manage → Manage tickets** page. An event can have
multiple ticket types (for example General Admission $10 and VIP $25).

## Adding / editing a ticket type

Click **"Add ticket"** (or **Edit** on an existing one) and set:

- **Name** — e.g. General Admission, VIP, Early Bird.
- **Description** — optional.
- **Type** — Paid or Free.
- **Price (USD)** — for paid tickets; set to $0 / Free for free tickets.
- **Quantity** — the number available. **0 = unlimited.**
- **Max per person** — the most one person can buy at once (blank = no limit).
- **Valid from / Valid until** — optional **scan window**: when the ticket can be
  scanned at the door. Tickets can still be bought beforehand; sales just close when the
  window ends.

A small service fee is added on top of your price and paid by the customer — you
receive the full amount you set.

## Reordering ticket tiers (drag to reorder)

- When a section has more than one ticket, a **drag handle** (the dotted grip on the
  left of each row) appears. **Drag the handle to change the order buyers see** in the
  app.
- The new order saves automatically when you drop the row ("Saving order…" appears
  briefly). If a save fails, the list snaps back to the previous order and shows an
  error — just try again.

## Marking a ticket sold out (without changing quantity)

- Each ticket row has a **"Mark sold out"** button. This forces that tier sold out
  immediately — buyers see a "Sold out" badge and can't purchase it — **without
  touching the quantity**.
- To reopen sales, click **"Mark available"** on that tier.
- Separately, a tier also shows "Sold out" **automatically** once its quantity sells
  through; to sell more in that case, increase the quantity.

## Hiding a ticket

- **"Hide"** removes a ticket type from what buyers can purchase, while keeping it on
  the event. Hidden tickets move to a "Hidden tickets" section.
- **Hidden tickets cannot be purchased, but existing ticket holders can still scan in.**
- Click **"Unhide"** to make it purchasable again.

## Quick tips

- Each row shows price, quantity (or "Unlimited"), and how many have **Sold** so far.
- Reordering, hiding, and mark-sold-out are separate controls — reordering only changes
  display order, hiding removes it from sale, and mark-sold-out blocks new purchases.
