# Team members & roles

There are **two different kinds of team** on Bizzy:

- **Business team** — people who work for your business ongoing (managers, staff,
  promoters). Set up from the **Team** page. They get dashboard access.
- **Event team (co-hosts)** — people helping with **one specific event only**. Set up
  inside that event (Manage → team). They do **NOT** get full dashboard access.

## Business team roles

- **Owner** — full access to everything, including settings and billing.
- **Manager** — create and manage events, deals, and line skips; view analytics; **no**
  business settings.
- **Staff** — scan tickets, sell via Tap-to-Pay, view basic event info.
- **Promoter** — view their share link and promotion performance.

## Global vs. venue-specific

- **Global** — the member works across all your venues.
- **Venue-specific** — the member only sees one venue.
- If you have one venue, everyone sees the same thing.

## Inviting a member

1. Go to **Team** in the sidebar and click **"Invite member"**.
2. Enter their email address.
3. Pick their **role** and **venue assignment**.

They'll get an email invitation. Once accepted, they have ongoing access based on their
role. **Only owners can manage business team members.**

## Common gotchas

- If a manager or staff member hits a "only the event owner or co-host can do this"
  message, they likely need to be added to that specific event's team (co-host), not
  just the business team — the two are separate.
