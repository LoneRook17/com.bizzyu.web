# Promoters & commissions

Promoters help promote your event and earn commission on every ticket they sell.
They get a personal share link, and you can see exactly who's driving sales.

## Turning on the Promoter Program for an event

1. When **creating or editing an event**, enable the **Promoter Program** and set the
   commission terms.
2. A user becomes a promoter by tapping **"Get paid to promote"** in the Bizzy app and
   accepting the terms — there's **no upfront phone verification and no Stripe setup**
   required to start.
3. Each promoter gets a **personalized share link**.
4. Visit the event's **Manage → Promoters** to see clicks, sales, and commission earned.

## Commission terms

- Commission can be a **percentage** or a **fixed amount** per ticket, set when you
  enable the program.
- The program is limited to **paid tickets** and requires the host to have **Stripe
  Connect** set up.
- Commission **comes out of the host's share** of each sale, based on the terms you set —
  it is not added on top of the ticket price.
- The commission terms you set are **snapshotted** onto each promoter's link, so editing
  the event later doesn't retroactively change what an existing promoter earns.
- A promoter earns **no commission on their own purchases** made through their own link.

## How promoters get paid (the wallet)

- Commissions **accrue to the promoter's in-app wallet** as their referrals buy tickets.
- Earnings start as **Pending** and stay pending until **shortly after the event ends**
  (a buffer to cover potential chargebacks), then become **Available**.
- Promoters **withdraw on demand** from the app whenever their Available balance reaches
  the **$20 minimum**. A **standard withdrawal is free**; an **instant withdrawal** costs
  a small fee.
- A promoter connects **Stripe at their first withdrawal** — not at signup.
- There is **no weekly payout** — promoters choose when to cash out.

## Notes

- Promoter earnings are separate from your own event revenue — commission comes out of
  the host's share, and the promoter cashes out from their own wallet to their own Stripe
  account.
