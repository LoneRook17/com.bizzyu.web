# Promoters & commissions

Promoters sign up to promote your event and earn commission on every ticket they sell.
They get a personal share link, and you can see exactly who's driving sales.

## Turning on the Promoter Program for an event

1. When **creating or editing an event**, enable the **Promoter Program** and set the
   commission terms.
2. Users tap **"Get paid to promote"** in the Bizzy app, verify their phone, and link
   Stripe.
3. Each promoter gets a **personalized share link**.
4. Visit the event's **Manage → Promoters** to see clicks, sales, and commission earned.

## Commission terms

- Commission can be a **percentage** or a **fixed amount** per ticket, set when you
  enable the program.
- The program is limited to **paid tickets** and (commission caps aside) requires the
  host to have **Stripe Connect** set up.
- The commission terms you set are **snapshotted** onto each promoter's link, so editing
  the event later doesn't retroactively change what an existing promoter earns.

## Payouts to promoters

- Commissions **accrue automatically** as the promoter's referrals buy tickets.
- Promoters are **paid out weekly via Stripe Connect** — you don't pay them manually.

## Notes

- Promoter payouts are separate from your own event revenue — the promoter's Stripe
  account receives their commission directly.
