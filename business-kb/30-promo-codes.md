# Promo codes

Promo codes offer discounts on tickets — great for promoters, VIPs, or early
supporters.

## Creating a promo code

1. Go to your event → **Manage → Promo codes → "Create promo code"**.
2. Set the **code** customers type at checkout (e.g. `FRIENDS50`).
3. Choose **percentage-off** or **fixed-amount-off**.
4. Set the **max number of uses**.

## How discounts and fees interact

- The service fee is calculated on the **discounted** price, not the original price.
- Codes apply at checkout in the app when the customer enters the code.

## Notes

- Promo codes are managed **per event** from that event's Manage page.
- Deals (the free foot-traffic offers) are separate from ticket promo codes — see the
  "Deals" article.
