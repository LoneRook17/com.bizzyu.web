## What you're buying

Door Access is entry to a venue for one specific night. Venues that run the same night
every week set it up as a Weekly Cover, and each week's night is sold separately. You buy
the night you want to go to, not a pass for the whole run.

- It covers **that night only**. Buying Thursday doesn't get you in on Friday.
- After buying, the QR is in your **Wallet tab** and in the receipt email.
- 21+, same as other nightlife products.

## Tiers

A night can offer more than one option, for example a standard door price and a
faster-entry option. What each one includes is set by the venue and shown at checkout.
Read what's included before buying, because it varies by venue.

If a tier is called something like "Skip the Line", that is an option within that night's
Door Access. It is not the same thing as Bizzy's standalone line-skip product, which is
bought from a venue's line-skip listing. If someone isn't sure what they bought, the
Wallet tab shows what it actually is.

## Refunds

Same as tickets: all sales are final, unless the venue cancels the night. If the venue
cancels, buyers are refunded automatically. Don't promise a date for the money to land,
that's between their bank and Stripe.

## If the QR won't scan

Escalate. Get the email on the account, the venue, and the night. Don't try to diagnose
QR problems from chat, and never tell someone they'll be let in anyway, because you can't
make that happen.
