## What Weekly Cover is

Weekly Cover is a recurring door program for a venue's regular nights. You set it up once
with the nights of the week it runs on, and Bizzy generates each upcoming night for you.
Customers buy **Door Access** for one specific night and show a QR at the door.

It is the same idea as a line skip in that it is a door product bought ahead of time, but
it is set up as a **series with a weekday template** rather than as a standalone schedule,
and the tiers are shared across every night in the series.

## How it is structured

- The **series** holds the template: which weekdays it runs, the door times, and the
  tiers that every night inherits.
- Each **night** is generated from that template. A night can be edited on its own, and
  once it is, it is marked as customized and stops following later template edits.
- Editing the **template** changes future nights that have not been customized. It does
  not rewrite a night you have already edited by hand, and it does not change what
  existing buyers paid.

## Tiers

A Weekly Cover night can have more than one tier, for example a standard cover and a
faster-entry tier. Tiers are defined on the template and apply to every night unless a
night overrides them.

Two things worth being explicit about:

- A tier named **"Skip the Line"** inside Weekly Cover is a **tier**, not a Bizzy line
  skip. It is bought as Door Access for that night and scanned the Weekly Cover way. The
  standalone line-skip product still exists separately, with its own screen in the
  sidebar and its own scanning method. If an owner says "skip the line", find out which
  of the two they mean before answering. See "Line skips & deals".
- Changing a tier's price affects future purchases only. Anyone who already bought keeps
  what they paid.

## At the door

Door Access QRs are scanned by staff. Confirm the scanning method against the shipped
build before this article goes out, because the door products do not all scan the same
way, and sending staff to the wrong scanner on a busy night is a real cost.

## Cancelling a night

Cancelling a Weekly Cover night refunds that night's buyers, the same way cancelling any
other night does. If there is money to refund, the cancellation goes to the Bizzy team for
review first; if there is nothing refundable, it cancels immediately. See "Events".

## What not to say

- Don't describe Weekly Cover as a subscription or a membership. A customer buys one
  night at a time.
- Don't promise that a night will be generated by a particular date. Say the series
  generates upcoming nights automatically and point the owner at the series screen.
